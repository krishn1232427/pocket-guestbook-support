A guestbook with a little more heart

  1. Make an occasion. Pick a name, date, theme, and message length.
  2. Pass the spotlight. Guests choose a prompt, record for 15, 30, or 60 seconds, then review or retake.
  3. Keep the feeling. Export a keepsake movie with a title card and your saved messages.

Your memories stay in your hands

No account, ads, or analytics. Recordings stay on your device until you choose to share them. Back up important memories before deleting the app.
